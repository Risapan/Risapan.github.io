About Me

WHO I AM

I build AI that runs on your own machine and the products around it.

I'm a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the University of Ruhuna (25th batch). I like owning a feature end to end: the UI, the API, the database, the container and the CI pipeline that ships it.

Most of my recent work is practical AI that stays on-device: local LLMs through Ollama, Whisper speech-to-text, TTS and computer vision, wired into real apps that are fast, private and work offline. Underneath that, I enjoy systems work like sockets, concurrency, protocols and auth.
